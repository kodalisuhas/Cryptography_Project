# Skill: Debugging

## Purpose

Investigate and resolve bugs without introducing unrelated changes.

---

# Workflow

1. Reproduce the issue.
2. Identify root cause.
3. Explain why it happened.
4. Recommend the smallest possible fix.
5. Verify the fix.
6. Identify side effects.

---

Never redesign the system while fixing a bug.

Focus only on the reported issue.
